Missing products are canceled. Highlighted cells show changes; open product details for source lines and pricing exceptions. No currency conversion is performed.

FCV — Full Cancellation Value

Manual calculator for KMOD RevRec. Full cancellation, reduction (= swap), or DPP fee line. Daily rate uses a 360-day year; months use a flat 30-day month.

Term is the reduced line's own end date minus the effective date (flat 30-day month) — not the line's original term length. Swap: use the removed side only.

Term anchors to the effective date (fallback: ALI start date) through the ALI's own end date. A future-dated ALI is not active and is excluded.
